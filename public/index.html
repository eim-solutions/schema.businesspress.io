    Download the ZIP

    Download and unzip the extension.

  2. 2
    Open Chrome extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.

  3. 3
    Load the folder

    Click “Load unpacked” and select the extracted folder.

  4. 4
    Check a page

    Pin SEOMarkup, open a page and click the extension.

Know the limits.

  • Use Google Rich Results Test or Search Console to confirm rich-result eligibility.
  • Some server-side, blocked, proxied or unfamiliar trackers may not appear.
  • Declared preview images load directly from their source only when you select that option or request them in the Social tab.
  • Share links contain a compressed report after the # in the URL. Anyone with the link can read it.
  • Findings describe markup, not legal compliance, ranking impact or user intent.
