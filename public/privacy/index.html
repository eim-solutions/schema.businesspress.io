Privacy policy · Effective 10 August 2026

Your page data stays in your browser.

SEOMarkup inspects the active Chrome tab only after you click the extension. The extension does not send page data, browsing history, scan results, identifiers, or usage events to BusinessPress or any third party.

Extension data

  • All analysis happens locally in Chrome.
  • No scan history is stored.
  • No page content is uploaded or transmitted.
  • No accounts, advertising identifiers, analytics events, or telemetry are used.
  • Clipboard and JSON export actions happen only after you explicitly select them.

Permissions

activeTab grants temporary access to the page after a toolbar click. scripting runs the bundled analyzer in that tab. SEOMarkup requests no host permissions and cannot inspect websites continuously in the background.

Website data

The public website contains no analytics, advertising pixels, account system, or contact form. Standard infrastructure logs may temporarily record technical request details such as IP address, requested path, browser user agent, and timestamp for security and reliable delivery.

Limits

Chrome blocks extension access to browser-internal pages, the Chrome Web Store, and some protected surfaces. Tracker detection covers evidence visible in rendered markup and the browser Performance API; it does not prove that a page is tracker-free.

Contact

SEOMarkup is published by BusinessPress. Privacy enquiries can be sent through businesspress.io.
