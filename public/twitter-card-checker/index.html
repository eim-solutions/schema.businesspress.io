What this check covers

The preview follows declared metadata, while making clear that X may apply its own cache, crop and presentation rules.

  • twitter:card type
  • twitter:title and twitter:description
  • twitter:image and image alt text
  • Fallbacks to Open Graph metadata

Choose the card type deliberately

A summary card and a large-image card create different emphasis. Confirm the declared type fits the content.

Keep copy concise

Long titles and descriptions can be shortened by the platform, so put the important meaning first.

Review fallback behavior

When Twitter-specific fields are absent, platforms may use Open Graph values instead.
