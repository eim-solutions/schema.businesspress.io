Questions about open graph checker

What are Open Graph tags?

They are page metadata used by social platforms and messaging tools to build link previews.

Does LinkedIn use Open Graph data?

LinkedIn commonly reads Open Graph fields when it builds a shared-link preview.

Why is my old Open Graph image still showing?

A platform cache may still hold an earlier version even after the source metadata changes.
